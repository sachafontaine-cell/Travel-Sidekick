Travel Sidekick · Privacy

Travel Sidekick is a private app made for one family. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

  • Your trips are stored on your phones and in a private Google Sheet in your own Google Drive.
  • Your Google script runs in your own Google account. It reads booking confirmation emails for your trip dates (read-only), sends you alert emails, and keeps the Sheet.
  • Gemini (Google's AI) receives a short summary of the trip (destination, dates, ages, preferences) and, for bookings, email text with card, passport, phone and reference numbers removed. On Google's free tier, Google may use this data to improve its products.
  • Other services: weather from Open-Meteo, places from OpenStreetMap, exchange rates from Frankfurter. They receive only a city or place name.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. To delete everything: delete the trip in the app, the Sheet in your Drive, and the script.
